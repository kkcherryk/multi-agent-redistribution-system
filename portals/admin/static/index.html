Simulation and evaluation

Runs a pretend city (not your real data) and compares four ways of choosing a collector. The same seed always gives the same city, food posts and agent behaviour, so every strategy faces exactly the same days.

Bold = best value. "±" = spread across cities. Times and distances are averaged over saved food only, so a strategy that rescues harder (farther) food can show longer times. "Our agents (no trust)" is the same as "Our agents" with the trust penalty switched off, to show what trust adds.

Trust scores

Decision support only: scores never block anyone automatically. Score = (successes + 1) / (total + 2) × 100; a new account starts at 50 with low confidence.
