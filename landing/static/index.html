About the project

The Multi-Agent Food Redistribution System uses trust-aware negotiation and reinforcement learning to move surplus food. It brings restaurants and NGOs together through intelligent coordination, so surplus food reaches people while it is still fresh and safe.

Our vision

No good meal is thrown away while someone nearby goes hungry.

Our approach

Software agents that weigh trust, distance and time, and learn to dispatch better with every pickup.

How we work to make a difference

From a restaurant counter to a hungry family — in seven simple steps.

  1. 🍲Food listedA restaurant posts its surplus
  2. 🔎Needs foundAgents look for NGOs that can use it
  3. 🤝NegotiationTrust, distance and time are weighed
  4. ✅AssignedThe best NGO gets the donation
  5. 🛵On the wayPickup is coordinated and tracked
  6. 📦CollectedFood is checked and received
  7. ❤️DeliveredIt reaches people who need it

Not just a listing site.
A team of smart agents.

Behind every pickup, small software agents talk to each other — like a coordinator who never sleeps.

  • Restaurant agents announce new food
  • NGO agents say what they can collect
  • Logistics agents plan the quickest pickup
  • Storage & backup agents step in if a pickup fails
  • Trust-aware negotiation favours reliable partners
  • Reinforcement learning improves every dispatch
